I’m Lee Button. I’m interested in how we work together, what helps people stay well, and how good ideas become useful in everyday life.

This is my personal home for thinking out loud. Work is part of it. So are books, media, bags, gear and whatever else catches my attention.

What I keep coming back to

  • Working together

    Shared responsibility, systems and the practical work of making progress when no one person holds all the answers.

  • Creating health

    The places, services and everyday conditions that shape our health, and how we might make them better.

  • Everyday curiosities

    Things I read, watch, carry and use. Small observations, useful objects and ideas worth following.
